Dental Fillings

Dental fillings are used to repair teeth damaged by decay or other injuries. They restore the tooth's shape, function, and strength. Common filling materials include amalgam, composite, gold, and porcelain.

Why are Fillings Necessary?

  • Treating Cavities: Fillings are the primary treatment for cavities, sealing the tooth to prevent further decay.
  • Repairing Damage: Fillings can also repair chipped, cracked, or broken teeth.
  • Preventing Further Issues: By sealing the tooth, fillings prevent bacteria from entering and causing more extensive damage.

Procedure:

  • Numbing: The area around the affected tooth is numbed with a local anesthetic.
  • Decay Removal: The decayed portion of the tooth is removed using a drill, air abrasion instrument, or laser.
  • Filling Placement: The chosen filling material is placed into the prepared space and shaped to restore the tooth's original form.

Discuss Your Options With Our Dentist
